Draft — pending legal reviewThe summaries below describe our intended approach. They are not yet final policies and may change after review by our lawyers.

Privacy policy

The final policy will explain what personal data ADAIG collects, why we collect it and how long we keep it. It is expected to cover US federal and state privacy laws (for example CCPA/CPRA and other state acts), GDPR for members in the EU and UK, marketing consent under CAN-SPAM, how to make a data request and our response times, the service providers (processors) we use, and where data is stored.

In the meantime, anyone with an account can download or delete their data from Profile & privacy after signing in. Messages sent through our contact form (your name, email, optional organization and message) are used only to respond to you, and are deleted along with your account if you delete it. For any other privacy request, use the contact form with the topic “Privacy request”.

Terms of use

ADAIG is based in the United States. The governing law and venue for these terms have not yet been decided; the final terms will set them out.

Marketing email

We will send marketing email only to people who opt in. Every message will identify ADAIG, will include ADAIG’s postal address and a working unsubscribe link, and opt-outs will be honored within 10 business days, as required by CAN-SPAM.

Accessibility statement

ADAIG aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA, in line with ADA and Section 508 expectations. If something on this site is hard to use, please tell us through our contact form and we will work with you to provide the information in another way.

Cookies

The site uses essential cookies to keep you signed in securely. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics, we will use a privacy-friendly service, and non-essential cookies will stay off unless you agree to them.
