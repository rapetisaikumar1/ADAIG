About ADAIG

The Principles That Guide Us

The commitments that shape how ADAIG serves its members and the wider community.

Our principles

  1. Trust and Transparency

    Promoting accountable, explainable, and ethical use of data and AI.

  2. Independence

    Maintaining objective, credible governance guidance free from undue commercial influence.

  3. Privacy and Human Oversight

    Protecting individual rights and ensuring meaningful human accountability.

  4. Inclusion and Collaboration

    Making governance education and professional advancement accessible across industries and communities.
