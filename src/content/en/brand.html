Brand direction

Credible, modern, trustworthy — an institution with a practitioner’s pulse.

Logo: bilingual lockup

The primary logo is a bilingual wordmark “ADAIG · AGDIA”. English is set in navy and French in teal, joined by an amber interpunct, so both official languages have equal weight. The tile mark is a serif “A” with a teal baseline (the governed foundation) and an amber dot (the human in the loop), repeating the interpunct.

Primary = Variant A (horizontal). The optional descriptor line gives the full English and French names.

Clear space = the tile’s amber dot diameter ×2.
Min size 160px horizontal / 16px mark.
Don’t recolour, re-order the languages or separate the interpunct.

Voice & tone

  • Authoritative, not academic. Plain language; define jargon.
  • Practical. Lead with what the reader can do.
  • Even-handed. No hype about AI; no fear either.
  • Inclusive & global. US English spelling, bilingual-ready (EN/FR), globally relevant.
  • Transparent. Disclose AI use, sponsors and affiliations.
