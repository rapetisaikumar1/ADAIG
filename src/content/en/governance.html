Board & Governance

Leadership Built on Experience, Integrity, and Foresight

ADAIG is guided by leaders and industry experts committed to advancing responsible data and AI governance.

Our leadership combines strategic oversight, professional expertise, and forward-looking perspectives to strengthen governance standards, anticipate emerging risks, and empower organizations and professionals to navigate an evolving digital future.

Through transparent governance, independent decision-making, and collaboration, ADAIG strives to build a trusted professional community dedicated to responsible innovation and long-term resilience.

Code of ethics

Our professional commitments

Draft

These commitments put into practice The Principles That Guide Us: trust and transparency, independence, privacy and human oversight, and inclusion and collaboration.

The code of ethics is in draft. The commitments listed here set out its structure; the full clause text is being drafted and will be published once adopted.
  1. Act with integrity and accountability
  2. Protect privacy and the rights of data subjects
  3. Ensure meaningful human oversight of AI
  4. Disclose conflicts of interest
  5. Maintain professional competence (CPE)
