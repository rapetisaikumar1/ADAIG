Questions

Membership FAQ

How do I join?

Choose a tier and submit an application — there is no online payment. Our team reviews every application, and your point of contact will be in touch to confirm your membership and dues.

How much does membership cost?

Membership dues are being finalized by the founding board. Your point of contact will confirm the dues for your tier, and the available payment options, before your membership starts.

Is there a member directory?

Yes. Active members can search the member directory by name, job title and organization after signing in. Listing is opt-in, and you can change it at any time in your profile.

Is there a free option?

Yes. A free account lets you register for events at non-member rates and follow your registrations from your account. You can apply for membership at any time.

How does renewal work?

Your renewal date is shown in your account. Renewal isn’t handled online: once the date passes, My membership shows that renewal is due and our team will contact you. A membership that isn’t renewed within the grace period lapses.

What does enterprise membership offer my organization?

Enterprise membership is being designed around shared intelligence, workforce capability-building, peer learning and evolving governance resources, to help your organization navigate uncertainty and scale AI responsibly. Several of these are planned or proposed rather than available today, and pricing and seat benefits are to be confirmed. Contact us and our team will be in touch.

Do you offer bespoke assessments or advisory work?

Not yet. Structured assessments and advisory engagements are proposed for the future and are not currently offered. Register your interest through the contact form.
