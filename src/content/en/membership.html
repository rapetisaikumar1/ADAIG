Enterprise membership

Govern Today. Anticipate Tomorrow. Thrive Beyond.

Enterprise membership helps your organization govern data and AI under uncertainty — building the shared intelligence, workforce capability and foresight needed for responsible scaling.

Shared intelligence

Collective insight from practitioners across sectors on emerging risks, regulatory signals and what is working in practice — so you anticipate change rather than react to it.

Workforce capability-building

Webinars, CPE-ready events and shared resources that raise governance maturity across your teams, not just for a few specialists.

Peer learning

Working groups and member exchanges where governance leaders compare approaches, test assumptions and build resilience together.

Evolving governance resources

Access to a growing library of frameworks, templates and guidance that is updated as standards, regulation and technology move.

Questions

Membership FAQ

How do I join?

Choose a tier and submit an application — there is no online payment. Our team reviews every application, and your point of contact will be in touch to confirm your membership and dues.

How much does membership cost?

Membership dues are being finalized by the founding board. Your point of contact will confirm the dues for your tier, and the available payment options, before your membership starts. Nothing is charged when you apply.

Is there a free option?

Yes — a free guest account gives access to public resources and event registration at non-member rates.

How does renewal work?

We plan to send reminders 30 and 7 days before renewal, with a 30-day grace period (to be confirmed).

What does enterprise membership offer my organization?

Shared intelligence, workforce capability-building, peer learning and access to evolving governance resources — helping your organization navigate uncertainty and scale AI responsibly. Pricing and seat benefits are to be confirmed; contact us and our team will be in touch.

Do you offer bespoke assessments or advisory work?

Not yet. Structured assessments and advisory engagements are proposed for the future and are not currently offered. Register your interest via contact.
