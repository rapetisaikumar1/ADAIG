Actions

Buttons

One shape (pill), 44px minimum. Hover changes color only.

With arrow
Text link with arrow
  • Highlight (amber): membership actions only (Join, Apply, Become a member): the header Join button plus at most one amber button on the page.
  • Primary (navy): the main action of a page or panel. Accent (teal): form submits. Secondary/Ghost: everything else.
  • Loading: aria-busy="true" on the button keeps its label and adds a spinner.
Status

The status component

One outline pill with a 6px marker and one tone per meaning: four public tones, plus pending, neutral and danger in the portals. At most one status per card.

Available nowComing soonPlannedProposedMembers onlyPending reviewNo membershipRejectedWebinarPriority3
  • Available live, active, published, paid · Coming soon planned tone · Planned / Proposed / Draft in development · Pending needs someone’s action · Neutral factual, no action · Danger rejected, canceled, locked.
  • Legacy classes map onto the same look: .badge-green/.badge-teal → available, .badge-soon → planned, .badge-amber → in development, .badge-navy → members/category, .badge-red → danger.
