About ADAIG

Our mission

We exist to raise the standard of data, records and AI governance — through credentials, learning, community and principled advocacy.

Why ADAIG

Organisations are deploying AI faster than they can govern it. The professionals accountable for data, records and privacy need shared standards, peers and a credible voice. ADAIG brings these disciplines together.

ADAIG should model what it teaches: transparent governance, privacy by design, and human oversight of any AI it uses.

Vision

The home for professionals who govern data, records and AI.

Privacy-first

Built for US privacy laws (state privacy acts such as the CCPA/CPRA) and usable globally (GDPR).

Independent

Clear conflict-of-interest controls for vendors and sponsors.

Inclusive

WCAG 2.2 AA, bilingual-ready, affordable student entry.
