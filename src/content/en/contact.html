FAQ

Who can join?

Anyone working in, or studying, data, records, privacy or AI governance. See membership for the options.

What happens after I apply?

The ADAIG team reviews your application and your point of contact will be in touch about next steps. No payment is taken when you apply.

How quickly will you reply to my message?

Messages sent through this form go straight to the ADAIG team, who reply by email. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Do you offer French services?

The site navigation and forms are available in French (use the FR switch), and you are welcome to write to us in French. French versions of some page content are still being prepared.

How do I request my data?

Members can download their data (JSON or CSV) or delete their account from Profile → Privacy after signing in. For any other privacy request, choose “Privacy request” as the topic in the form.

Where is my data stored?

Where data is stored, and the service providers we use, will be set out in our privacy policy, which is being finalized and is pending legal review.
