FAQ

Who can join?

Anyone working in, or studying, data, records, privacy or AI governance. See membership for the options.

What happens after I apply?

We email you to confirm we’ve received your application. The ADAIG team then reviews it, and your point of contact will be in touch about next steps. No payment is taken when you apply. You can follow the status of your application under My membership, and we’ll email you once it has been reviewed.

How quickly will you reply to my message?

Messages sent through this form go straight to the ADAIG team, who reply by email. You’ll receive an automatic confirmation when your message arrives, and we aim to respond within a few business days.

I forgot my password. What can I do?

Select “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page, or go straight to reset your password. Enter the email you sign in with and we’ll email you a link to choose a new password. The link works once and expires after 60 minutes. Still stuck? Send us a message with “Account & sign-in” as the topic.

How do I request my data?

Anyone with an account can download their data (JSON or CSV) or delete their account from Profile & privacy after signing in. For any other privacy request, send us a message with “Privacy request” as the topic.

Where is my data stored?

Where data is stored, and the service providers we use, will be set out in our privacy policy, which is being finalized and is pending legal review.
