Enterprise foresight working group

A proposed forum that brings AI governance, privacy, records, risk, legal and strategy professionals into the same conversation, to anticipate emerging risks together rather than in silos.

  • Share early signals on regulatory, technology and operational change
  • Discuss how to scale AI and data use responsibly across functions
  • Explore practices that build organizational resilience
Not yet launched. Scope and format will be shaped by those who register interest.Register interest

Why cross-functional?

Emerging risks rarely stay inside one discipline. An AI deployment can raise privacy, records retention, legal and enterprise-risk questions at once. Interest groups go deep within a discipline; working groups are intended to connect them.

If you would like to help shape a working group, or propose a different theme, get in touch.
