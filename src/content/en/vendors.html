How listings will work

Planned

Not endorsements

A listing tells you a provider exists and what it offers. It is not a recommendation or an assessment of quality.

Affiliations disclosed

Any connection between a vendor and ADAIG’s board, advisors or staff will be labeled on the listing, in line with our conflict-of-interest commitments.

Sponsors labeled

Sponsored listings will be clearly marked, so you can always tell paid placement from a standard listing.
